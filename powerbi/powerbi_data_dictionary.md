## 1. Overview & Provenance Classification

All data utilized in the CareFlow Clinical Pathway project originates from a controlled synthetic event simulator designed to mimic typical acute emergency hospital workflows. No real patient Protected Health Information (PHI) or Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems were accessed.

Attributes within the semantic model carry one of two provenance tags:
1. **`OBSERVED_BASELINE` (`SIMULATED`):** Data generated by the probabilistic clinical pathway simulator (`src/data_generator.py`) and normalized via dbt/Python, representing the current hospital operational workflow (As-Is baseline).
2. **`SIMULATED_ESTIMATE` (`HYPOTHETICAL`):** Data produced by the As-Is vs. To-Be scenario improvement engine (`src/pathway_improvement.py`), modeling a proposed future state with hypothetical re-triage queue consolidation and 15% coordination reductions.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Operational Interpretation Warning:**
> - **Transition Duration vs. Waiting Time:** `transition_minutes_from_previous` records total elapsed clock time between successive milestone timestamps. It must not be interpreted as pure patient waiting room idle time, as the event log records discrete milestone completion timestamps rather than unmeasured internal sub-processes.
> - **Clinical Safety Assumption:** In the To-Be scenario, repeated triage events are removed as a hypothetical operational improvement. Simulated event logs cannot determine whether reassessment was clinically necessary due to patient clinical deterioration. Qualified medical staff must validate this assumption before clinical implementation.

## 2. Table: `FactHospitalEvents`

- **Source File:** `data/cleaned/stg_hospital_events.csv`
- **Cloud Equivalent:** BigQuery VIEW `exalted-mode-467509-h1.careflow_staging.stg_hospital_events`
- **Granularity:** One record per recorded clinical or administrative event in a patient encounter.
- **Row Count:** 3,140 rows.
- **Encounter Volume:** 500 unique cases (`CASE_0001` through `CASE_0500`).

| Field Name | Data Type | Nullable | Provenance | Description & Business Rules | Example Values |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `case_id` | `STRING` | No | `OBSERVED_BASELINE` | Unique patient encounter identifier. Primary key for tracing patient journeys. | `CASE_0001`, `CASE_0245` |
| `event_sequence` | `INTEGER` | No | `OBSERVED_BASELINE` | 1-indexed chronological sequence order of the event within the case journey (1 to 8). | `1`, `2`, `3` |
| `raw_activity_name` | `STRING` | No | `OBSERVED_BASELINE` | Proper-cased clinical or administrative activity name as originally recorded. | `Registration`, `Triage`, `Consultation`, `Laboratory`, `X-Ray`, `Treatment`, `Discharge` |
| `activity_name` | `STRING` | No | `OBSERVED_BASELINE` | Lowercase snake_case standardized activity identifier for uniform querying and join contracts. | `registration`, `triage`, `consultation`, `laboratory`, `x_ray`, `treatment`, `discharge` |
| `event_timestamp` | `DATETIME` | No | `OBSERVED_BASELINE` | Date and minute-precision time at which the clinical activity was logged. | `2024-10-01 08:12:00` |
| `event_date` | `DATE` | No | `OBSERVED_BASELINE` | Calendar date extracted from `event_timestamp` for daily census aggregation and calendar joins. | `2024-10-01`, `2024-10-02` |
| `department` | `STRING` | No | `OBSERVED_BASELINE` | Hospital physical department where the activity took place (`ER`, `LAB`, `RADIOLOGY`). | `ER`, `LAB`, `RADIOLOGY` |

| `previous_activity` | `STRING` | Yes | `OBSERVED_BASELINE` | Name of the immediately preceding activity in the patient journey. Null for initial `Registration`. | `Registration`, `Triage`, `Consultation` |

## 8. Summary of Data Integrity Contracts

1. **Uniqueness:**
   - `FactHospitalEvents` event uniqueness contract: `(case_id, event_sequence)` and `(case_id, raw_activity_name, event_timestamp)`.
   - `DimPatientCase`: `case_id` is unique across all 500 rows.
2. **Referential Integrity:**
   - 100% of `FactHospitalEvents[case_id]` values match an existing record in `DimPatientCase[case_id]`.
   - 100% of `DimPatientCase[observed_pathway]` values match an existing record in `DimPathwayVariants[pathway]`.
   - 100% of `FactHospitalEvents[event_date]` values match a date in `DimDate[Date]`.
3. **Parity Check:**
   - Metrics derived via DAX (`[Total Cases] = 500`, `[Total Events] = 3,140`, `[Avg Case Cycle Time] = 202.54 min`, `[Median Case Cycle Time] = 211.00 min`) exhibit 100% numerical match with Python PM4Py process mining outputs and BigQuery warehouse views.
